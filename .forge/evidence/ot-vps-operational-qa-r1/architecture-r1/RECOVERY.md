# Production recovery policy — outstanding approval and demonstration

The explicit current QA mission prohibits backups, snapshots and dumps. None was created. Current configuration rollback is not database recovery or disaster protection. Do not certify municipal production readiness from this host-hardening exercise.

Before municipal production, Engineer/LGU authority must approve actual RPO/RTO, retention, legal holds, backup processor/location, encryption and deletion policy, trusted access/custodians, alert recipient and restore acceptance criteria. A proposed engineering starting point for discussion is daily consistent encrypted off-host backups plus WAL archiving where approved, with RPO15minutes/RTO4hours; these are NOT approved municipal commitments. Size/measure the actual dataset and restore performance before accepting targets.

The eventual approved solution must cover PostgreSQL data/WAL, private document/broker bytes and authority continuity, APP_KEY and required private secrets, immutable source/image/package/registry receipts and infrastructure configuration. Keep encryption/recovery keys separately controlled from the VPS and backup storage; avoid a single compromised host granting both data and recovery-key access. Define least-privilege backup/restore identities, encrypted transport/storage, immutable retention where required, revocation, failure alerts and documented disposal. Do not copy plaintext credentials or MFA into ordinary logs/evidence.

Require a real timed restoration drill under separate explicit authority into an isolated approved recovery environment, with checksum/registry/custody/relationship/authentication verification and an incident/cutover decision. Preserve official records and replay boundaries; avoid duplicate real notifications or actors during recovery. Record measured RPO/RTO and independent acceptance. No drill, extra recovery instance or backup is authorized by this document for the present mission.

Other unawarded production gates include independent operational QA/Siris activation, actual current source deployment/least-privilege postconditions, municipal policy/privacy/scope acceptance, external on-call alert routing, approved patch/reboot/out-of-band recovery access and ongoing maintenance ownership. HOST_BASELINE_READY, READY_FOR_SIRIS_QA and MUNICIPAL_PRODUCTION_READY are distinct dispositions.
